  Photography Plan for The Restart                                                              
                                                                                                  
  The visual thesis                                                                               
                                                                                                  
  Every photo sits behind a dark teal (#193540) overlay. That means the photography doesn't need  
  to be literal — it needs to feel right when dimmed. Look for: strong directional light, a clear 
  focal point, not too busy. Images with a lot of mid-tone detail look muddy at 65% dim. Images   
  with strong light/shadow contrast survive the overlay beautifully.

  ---
  Homepage CTA band — "Build your registry. Start fresh."
                                                         
  Drop dim from 70% → 60% so the image actually shows through. At 70% you're just looking at a
  dark rectangle.                                                                                 
   
  What to look for:                                                                               
  - A woman opening a door or stepping through a doorway into light
  - Someone placing plants or arranging a new space, looking satisfied                            
  - An empty, sunlit apartment — the moment before a new chapter      
                                                                                                  
  Unsplash search terms:                                                                          
  - woman doorway light morning                                                                   
  - woman moving into apartment confident                                                         
  - empty apartment sunlight hardwood    
  - woman new home plants moving                                                                  
                                
  What to avoid: sad expressions, overly staged/stock-photo energy, cluttered scenes, anything    
  that reads "divorce" rather than "fresh start."                                                 
                                                                                                  
  ---                                                                                             
  Sub-page headers (Login, Register, Start a Registry, My Account, My Registries)
                                                                                                  
  These are only 220px tall — the image is mostly atmosphere. One image across all sub-pages keeps
   things coherent and saves you from hunting for 5 separate photos.                              
                  
  Best fit: An overhead/flat-lay of morning items on a light surface — journal, coffee, a few     
  plants, maybe keys. Or: a soft close-up of morning light on a windowsill with a plant. Something
   personal, quiet, and self-possessed.                                                           
                  
  Unsplash search terms:                                                                          
  - morning desk journal coffee flatlay
  - woman journaling morning light minimal                                                        
  - windowsill plant morning light soft   
  - aerial journal notebook coffee minimal
                                                                                                  
  Keep dim at 65%. Set the focal point in the center of the image so the 220px crop shows the most
   interesting part.                                                                              
                  
  ---                                                                                             
  Blog / article pages
                      
  Single posts: Each post should get a featured image set individually. When you write a post
  about legal tips, a photo of someone confidently reading documents. When you write about        
  rebuilding your social life, friends at a table. The cover block in the single post template
  will use the featured image automatically if you wire it up — or you can just use the post's    
  featured image in a header image block.

  Category archive headers: Same approach as sub-page headers — one strong image per category. For
   the main blog/articles category, something like:
  - woman reading couch light empowered                                                           
  - woman laptop coffee home confident                                                            
   
  ---                                                                                             
  How to add them in WordPress
                                                                                                  
  1. Download the image from Unsplash (free, no attribution required for personal/commercial use
  under Unsplash License)                                                                         
  2. Upload to WP Admin → Media → Add New
  3. Edit the page (or use Appearance → Editor for templates) → click the Cover block → in the    
  right sidebar under "Media," click "Replace" and select your uploaded image                     
  4. Set the focal point — drag the dot to the most important part of the image (usually a face or
   the brightest area)                                                                            
  5. Adjust the dim ratio — for the CTA band, bring it to 60%; sub-pages can stay at 65%
                                                                                                  
  For the sub-page templates (login, register, etc.) you'll do this via Appearance → Editor →     
  Templates in the block editor, since those are FSE templates.                                   
                                                                                                  
  ---             
  One thing to watch
                    
  The teal overlay is a strong brand element — keep it. Don't switch to a black overlay. The
  teal-over-photography look is distinctive and already part of what makes this site feel like    
  itself, not a generic WordPress theme. Just let the photography show through a bit more by
  reducing the dim ratio.
